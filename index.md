    <p>My name is <b>Sidharth Aroli-Veettil</b>, and I am a Ph.D. student at the <a href="https://astro.uc.cl/">Institute of Astrophysics</a>, <a href="https://www.uc.cl/">Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile</a>, in the <a href="https://vvguzman.com/">Astrochemistry Group</a>.</p>
    <p>I study the <b>chemistry of the UV-irradiated layers of planet-forming disks</b>: how carbon and nitrogen are processed there, how their isotopes (<sup>14</sup>N/<sup>15</sup>N, <sup>12</sup>C/<sup>13</sup>C) are fractionated, and what this means for the material that comets and planets inherit. I mainly use <b>ALMA</b> observations, and I am a member of the ALMA Large Program <b>DECO</b> (Disk-Exoplanet C/Onnection) and of the <b>YEMS</b> Millennium Nucleus on Young Exoplanets and their Moons.</p>
