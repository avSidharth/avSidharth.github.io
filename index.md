My name is **Sidharth Aroli-Veettil**, and I am a Ph.D. student at the [Institute of Astrophysics](https://astro.uc.cl/), [Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile](https://www.uc.cl/).

I am part of the [Astrochemistry Group at PUC](https://vvguzman.com/), where my research focuses on the **chemistry of the UV-irradiated layers of planet-forming disks**: how carbon and nitrogen are processed there, how their isotopes (<sup>14</sup>N/<sup>15</sup>N, <sup>12</sup>C/<sup>13</sup>C) are fractionated, and what this means for the material that comets and planets inherit. I use **ALMA** observations and molecular line analysis, and I am a member of the ALMA Large Program **DECO** (Disk-Exoplanet C/Onnection) and of the **YEMS** Millennium Nucleus on Young Exoplanets and their Moons.
